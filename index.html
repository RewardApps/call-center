Sandip (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides Scam Call Centre (“the app”), a fictional simulation game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us.

Current release and planned features

The current Android release, version 1.0.0, works offline. It does not include analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, online AI services or microphone recording. We plan to introduce analytics, ads and in-app purchases in future versions. The sections below explain the types of processing those features may involve; they do not mean that these features are active today.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen player name, settings, game progress, fictional balance, unlocked operators and game ledger locally on your device. We do not receive this information in the current offline release.

The current Android release disables automatic backup of its private app data. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes its local progress.

2. Information you provide to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request, investigate reported problems and maintain necessary support records.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, real security codes or other unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Voice playback and permissions

The current release plays bundled audio and may use your installed text-to-speech service to read synthetic game dialogue. Depending on your device and speech-service settings, that service may process game text online under its own privacy policy. The app does not send microphone recordings or your chosen player name to that service.

The current release does not request Internet, microphone, camera, contacts or location permissions. It does not send dialogue to Fish Audio or Groq.

4. Analytics — planned for future versions

Future versions may introduce analytics or crash-reporting services to understand feature usage, measure reliability and improve the game.

Depending on the service and configuration, information processed may include:

  • Game events, screen views and feature interactions.
  • App version, operating-system version and device model.
  • Session information and app-instance identifiers.
  • Crash reports, performance measurements and diagnostics.
  • IP addresses and approximate region derived from them.

Before activating these services, we will update this policy to identify the providers, describe the actual information processed and explain available privacy choices. Where required, collection will depend on your consent.

5. Advertising — planned for future versions

Future versions may display advertisements, including optional rewarded ads. Advertising providers may process device information, advertising or other permitted identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Depending on your region, eligibility, settings and consent, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

We will identify the advertising providers and their privacy policies before enabling ads. Where required, the app will provide a consent message and a way to revisit privacy choices. Device advertising controls may provide additional options.

6. In-app purchases — planned for future versions

Future versions may offer optional digital purchases through Google Play Billing. If introduced, Google Play will handle checkout and payment processing. We will not receive your full payment-card number or payment-account password.

The app, a verification backend or a disclosed purchase-service provider may process product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction references, purchase status and entitlement information to verify purchases, provide purchased content, restore eligible purchases and prevent fraud.

We will update this policy with the actual purchase-processing arrangements before introducing paid features. Applicable purchase, refund and subscription terms will be shown through the relevant store and purchase flow.

7. Third parties and sharing

The current offline app does not send personal information to us or to analytics, advertising or purchase SDKs. Your installed speech service, email provider and app-store platform operate under their own policies.

If future services are introduced, information may be processed by the providers identified in the updated policy for the purposes described above. We may also disclose information we hold when legally required or when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users or defend legal rights.

Information processed by future providers may be handled in countries other than your own. Relevant providers, processing arrangements and applicable safeguards will be described when those services are selected.

8. Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Support correspondence is kept only as reasonably necessary to address requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods for any future analytics, advertising or purchase records will depend on the purposes, provider configuration and applicable obligations. Those arrangements will be disclosed before the relevant features are activated.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to information we handle. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and requests

  • Clear Android app storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored game progress.
  • Manage your installed speech service through device settings.
  • If future consent-based services are introduced, use the privacy controls provided to review or withdraw consent.
  • Contact us about information contained in support correspondence.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, or to request a portable copy. We may need to verify a request before responding. We cannot retrieve local game data that has not been sent to us.

10. Children’s privacy

The current offline release does not collect personal information from players. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address the matter.

Any future analytics, advertising or purchase features will need to be configured for the app’s declared audience and applicable requirements. The store listing provides the app’s content rating and audience information.
